# BAC English Examination Specifications & Task Pool (Master Guide)

---

## 1. Foreign Languages Stream

### Stream Parameters
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Coefficient:** 5
* **Reading Passage Length:** 150 to 200 words
* **Total Score:** 20 points
  * **Part 1:** 14 pts (Comprehension: 7 pts, Text Exploration: 7 pts)
  * **Part 2:** 6 pts
* **Task Selection Rule:** Select exactly **5 Comprehension tasks** and **5 Text Exploration tasks**.

---

### Part 1 - Section A: Comprehension Tasks (Select 5)

#### Task 1: Text Type / Verification / Multiple Choice
* **Option 1A (Text Type / Format):**
  * **Formula 1:** "The text is a..." (`a: [option 1]`, `b: [option 2]`, `c: [option 3]`)
  * **Formula 2:** "The text is an extract from an email, a web article, or a newspaper article."
  * *Constraint:* Exactly 3 options provided across both formulas.
* **Option 1B (True / False):** "Say whether the following statements are true or false."
  * *Constraint:* 3 to 4 statements.
* **Option 1C (True / False with Correction):** "Say whether the following statements are true or false and correct the false ones."
  * *Constraint:* 3 to 4 statements.
* **Option 1D (Multiple Choice):** "Write the letter that corresponds to the right answer." OR "Choose the answer to complete each statement."
  * *Constraint:* No more than 4 sentences/items, and exactly 3 choices provided per item.

#### Task 2: Location / Sequence / Data Extraction
* **Option 2A (Paragraph Identification):** "Identify the paragraph in which the following ideas are mentioned."
  * *Constraint:* Target 2 ideas from 2 different paragraphs.
* **Option 2B (Sentence Ordering):** "Put the following ideas in the order they appear in the text."
  * *Constraint:* Exactly 3 to 4 ideas.
* **Option 2C (Table Completion):** "Fill in the following table with information from the text."
  * *Constraint:* Custom table structure depending on extracted data; requires exactly 4 items to be filled.

#### Task 3: Direct & Inferential Questions
* **Wording:** "Answer the following questions according to the text."
* **Constraint:** Exactly 3 to 4 questions, including exactly 1 inference question.

#### Task 4: Cohesive Markers
* **Wording:** "Find who or what the underlined words refer to in the text."
* **Constraint:** Exactly 2 to 4 target words; selection must not be limited to referents that immediately follow or precede.

#### Task 5: Macro-Comprehension
* **Option 5A (Title):**
  * **Formula 1:** "Copy the title you think is the most appropriate." (*Constraint:* Exactly 3 suggested titles provided)
  * **Formula 2:** "Give a title to the text." (*Constraint:* Open response; no suggestions provided)
* **Option 5B (General Idea):**
  * **Formula 1:** "Choose the general idea of the text." (*Constraint:* Exactly 3 options provided)
  * **Formula 2:** "Give the general idea of the text." (*Constraint:* Open response; no options provided)
* **Option 5C (Discourse Type):** "The text is... a) descriptive b) narrative c) argumentative d) prescriptive."
  * *Constraint:* Apply only if a single discourse type dominates.

---

### Part 1 - Section B: Text Exploration Tasks (Select 5)

#### Task 1: Lexis / Vocabulary
* **General Constraint:** Applies to 1A, 1B, and 1C — exactly 2 to 3 target items; paragraph numbers must be specified.
* **Option 1A (Synonyms / Antonyms):** "Find in the text words or phrases that are closest in meaning to..." OR "...opposite in meaning to..."
* **Option 1B (Matching Table):** "Match the following words with their corresponding synonyms / opposites / definitions."
  | Word | Synonym, opposite, or definition |
  |---|---|
  | [Target words chosen for task] | [Matching synonyms, opposites, or definitions] |
* **Option 1C (Definition Match):** "Find words in the text whose definitions follow:"

#### Task 2: Morphology
* **Option 2A (Chart Completion Table):** "Complete the chart as shown in the example."
  * *Constraint:* Exactly 1 solved example + 3 to 4 derived words from the text (5 to 6 rows total).
  | Verb | Noun | Adjective |
  |---|---|---|
  | [Solved Verb Example] | [Solved Noun Transformation] | [Solved Adjective Transformation] |
  | [Target Word 1] | [Gap] | [Gap] |
  | [Gap] | [Target Word 2] | [Gap] |
  | [Gap] | [Gap] | [Target Word 3] |
* **Option 2B (Affix Separation Table):** "Divide the following words into roots and affixes." OR "Give the opposites of the following words keeping the same root."
  * *Constraint:* Exactly 3 to 4 words.
  | Prefix | Root | Suffix |
  |---|---|---|

#### Task 3: Grammar Transformation & Mechanics
* **Option 3A (Sentence Rephrasing):** "Rewrite sentence B so that it means the same as sentence A." (*Constraint:* 2 to 3 sentences)
* **Option 3B (Sentence Combination):** "Combine each pair of sentences with one of the connectors provided. Make changes where necessary." (*Constraint:* 2 to 3 sentence pairs)
* **Option 3C (Verb Tense Conjugation):** "Give the correct form of the verbs in brackets." (*Constraint:* Passage with 4 verbs in brackets)
* **Option 3D (Question Formation):** "Ask questions which the underlined words answer." (*Constraint:* Max 2 questions)

#### Task 4: Phonology
* **Option 4A (Final /s/ Pronunciation Table):** "Classify the following words according to the pronunciation of the final /s/." (*Constraint:* 3 to 4 words)
  | /s/ | /z/ | /ɪz/ |
  |---|---|---|
* **Option 4B (Final /ed/ Pronunciation Table):** "Classify the following words according to the pronunciation of the final /ed/." (*Constraint:* 3 to 4 words)
  | /t/ | /d/ | /ɪd/ |
  |---|---|---|
* **Option 4C (Stressed Syllable Table):** "Classify the following words according to the stressed syllable." (*Constraint:* 3 to 4 words)
  | 1st syllable | 2nd syllable | 3rd syllable | 4th syllable |
  |---|---|---|---|
* **Option 4D (Syllable Count Table):** "Classify the following words according to the number of syllables." (*Constraint:* 3 to 4 words)
  | One syllable | Two syllables | Three syllables | Four or more syllables |
  |---|---|---|---|
* **Option 4E (Silent Letter):** "Circle or write the silent letter in each of the following words."
* **Option 4F (Rhyming Pairs):** "Match the pairs that rhyme." (*Constraint:* Exactly 2 pairs)

#### Task 5: Discourse & Cohesion
* **Option 5A (Gap Fill with List):**
  * **Formula 1:** "Fill in the gaps with words from the list given." (*Constraints:* 4 gaps, 4 words)
  * **Formula 2:** "Fill in the gaps with only 4 words from the list given." (*Constraints:* 4 gaps, 6 words in list)
* **Option 5B (Open Gap Fill):** "Fill in the gaps so that the text makes sense." (*Constraint:* 4 gaps, no words provided)
* **Option 5C (Paragraph Reordering):** "Reorder the following sentences to get a coherent passage." (*Constraint:* 4 statements)
* **Option 5D (Dialogue Completion):** "Imagine what A or B says, and complete the following dialogue." (*Constraint:* 3 missing utterances)

---

### Part 2: Written Expression (6 Points Total)
* **Task Choices:** Topic 1 (Guided, notes provided, 80-120 words) OR Topic 2 (Free, no notes provided, 80-120 words).
* **Scoring Rubric:**
  * Relevance (1.5 pts)
  * Coherence (1.0 pt)
  * Use of Linguistic Resources (2.0 pts)
  * Excellence (1.5 pts)
