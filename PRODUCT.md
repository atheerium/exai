# PRODUCT.md

## Users
Algerian English teachers (middle and secondary school, 1AM to 3AS) preparing structured exams. They work between classes on school laptops, desktops, and phones. Mixed tech comfort: the tool must work with zero technical setup and zero AI knowledge.

## Product purpose
Exam creation, lightened. Exaai generates the reading text, tasks, and marks from the exam structure; the teacher reviews, edits, replaces, and approves. Every generation is a proposal, never a silent overwrite.

## Brand
Deep forest green (Algeria-inspired), soft mint-tinted neutrals, friendly geometric touches. The brand signals calm guidance, not tech novelty.

## Tone
Warm, plain, encouraging. Sentence case everywhere. Errors state what happened and what to do next, without apologizing. Empty states invite action.

## Anti-references
Chat-style AI tools, dense enterprise admin panels, dark developer tools, neon-accent generators.

## Strategic principles
1. The teacher stays the author; the interface proposes.
2. Nothing technical leaks to teachers (no API keys, no prompts, no jargon).
3. Familiar patterns over invented ones; consistency is the affordance.
4. One accent used for actions and state only, never decoration.
